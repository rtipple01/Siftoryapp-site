Sign up

Pick your phone. No need to send us your email address.

Android

Google Play

  1. Join the tester group

    Use the Google account on your phone. On Google’s page, tap Join group. You’ll then see our welcome message saying “You’re in!”. Come back here for step 2.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Come back here and tap the button, then tap Become a tester on Google Play’s page.

    Become a tester

  3. Install Siftory

    Tap Install. It can take a few minutes after step 2 to appear. Please keep it for 14 days.

    Install from Google Play
iPhone

Apple TestFlight

  1. Get TestFlight

    Apple’s free app for testing apps before they launch. You need iOS 16.4 or newer.

    Get TestFlight

  2. Join Siftory

    Come back here and tap the button, then tap Accept in TestFlight.

    Join Siftory

  3. Install Siftory

    Tap Install in TestFlight. Updates arrive there too.

    Open in TestFlight

What we’d love you to try

No need to do it all at once. Tick these off over the two weeks, in any order.

  • A Sift

    Start a Sift and swipe: up to Keep, down to Trash, left for Later, right for Favourite. Hold, then swipe up to drop a photo into a Keep Collection.

    • ↑ Keep
    • ↓ Trash
    • ← Later
    • → Favourite

  • Review and Complete

    Check your decisions, then Complete. Nothing is deleted until you confirm, and deleted items go to your phone’s Recycle bin.

  • Collections and phone albums

    Make a Collection, link it to a phone album, and check it appears in your Gallery app after you Complete.

  • Media Roll

    Browse your camera roll newest first and tidy as you go: swipe up to keep, down to Trash.

  • Undo

    Change your mind on purpose. Undo should put the last decision back exactly as it was.

  • The intro tour

    Open Settings › Help: take the tour and tell us if anything is unclear or out of date.

What we ask

Google needs at least 12 testers to stay opted in for 14 days in a row before Siftory can launch. So the most helpful thing is simply staying in.

  • Stay in for 14 days

    Please stay opted in and keep the app installed for at least 14 days.

  • Use it now and then

    A few minutes here and there is plenty. There’s no target to hit.

  • Don’t opt out and back in

    Leaving the test, even briefly, restarts your 14 days from the beginning.

No forms to fill in, and no reviews needed.

Found a problem or have an idea?

Tell us straight from the app. It takes a minute, and every report helps.

  1. Contact support – open Settings (the cog on Home) and tap Contact support. It opens an email to us.
  2. Say what happened – what you were doing, and what happened that you didn’t expect.
  3. Your app version – already filled in for you. Just leave it there.
  4. Add a screenshot if you can. Please avoid screenshots that show personal photos.

Prefer your usual email app? Write to support@siftoryapp.com directly. Ideas and suggestions are just as welcome as problems.

Every report gets a reply, and is logged so you can see it fixed in a later update.

What happens next

Here’s how the test runs from today to launch.

  1. Today

    You join

    Opt in, install Siftory and have a first sift. Lifetime is already unlocked for you.

  2. During the test

    Updates arrive

    New versions come automatically through Google Play, with fixes for what testers report.

  3. After 14 days

    We apply for launch

    Once enough testers have stayed in for 14 days, we ask Google to let Siftory go public.

  4. Launch

    You keep Lifetime

    We plan to give every tester a free Siftory Lifetime code at launch, as a thank you.

Questions

Will this delete my photos?

Only what you confirm. Swiping down just puts an item in Siftory’s Trash. Nothing on your phone changes until you review and tap Complete, and Android asks you to allow it too. Deleted items go to your phone’s Recycle bin, where Android normally keeps them for around 30 days, so you can still get them back.

Does it upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your phone. There’s no account, and Siftory has no servers that store your photos or videos. The privacy policy has the details.

I can’t find the app on Google Play

Check that the Play Store on your phone is signed in to the same Gmail address you gave me. If you have more than one Google account, switch to that one in the Play Store.

Open the opt-in link and tap Become a tester first, then use the Google Play link on that page. If it still doesn’t show, wait a few minutes and try again. Still stuck? Email support@siftoryapp.com.

Can I use an iPhone?

Not yet. Siftory is Android only for now.
