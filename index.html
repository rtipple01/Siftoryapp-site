How it works

A few minutes here and there is all it takes. Stop whenever you like and carry on later.

  1. Choose what to sift

    Your unsorted media, one or more Collections, or a phone album such as Pictures or WhatsApp.

  2. Swipe each item

    Up to keep, down to trash, left for later, right for a favourite. Or tap the tab instead.

  3. Review your decisions

    See everything you decided in one place and change anything before it touches your phone.

  4. Complete

    Apply your albums and clear the Trash. Deleted items go to your phone’s Recycle bin, so you can still change your mind.

Everything you need to tidy up

Built for real camera rolls: thousands of photos, screenshots, videos and that one blurry shot you took twelve times.

↕

Sift

A focused sorting session. Use Quick Clean’s standard swipes, or put a Collection on each direction. Save your setup as a profile and make it your default.

▶

Media Roll

A fast alternative to your camera roll. Swipe left and right to browse, up to send to a Collection, down to Trash. Pinch or double-tap to zoom.

▦

Collections and phone albums

Group your media into Collections. Link them to real albums in your Gallery, either all of them or just the ones you choose.

✓

Review before anything is deleted

Nothing changes on your phone until you confirm in Review and Complete. Deleted items go to the Recycle bin, not straight into oblivion.

●

Private by design

Siftory works entirely on your phone. There’s no account to create, nothing is uploaded and there are no Siftory servers holding your photos.

A quick tour

A short walkthrough shows what each screen and button does. Find it again any time under Settings › Help.

Four directions. One decision at a time.

Each swipe is a decision, and each decision can be undone.

  • Up – Keep. Hold, then swipe up to drop it into one of up to four Keep Collections.
  • Down – Trash. Marked for deletion, but nothing is deleted until you complete.
  • Left – Later. Not sure? Come back to it another time.
  • Right – Favourite. For the ones you love.

Media Roll: your camera roll, but quicker.

Browse newest first, just like Gallery, and sort as you go.

  • Swipe left and right to browse.
  • Swipe up (or tap) to send it to your chosen Collection, such as “Beach day”.
  • Swipe down for Trash, with Undo for a few seconds.
  • Pinch or double-tap to zoom. Tap Done to review what you trashed.
