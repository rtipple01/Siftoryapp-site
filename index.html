You stay in control

Nothing is deleted until you say so

Swiping down doesn’t delete anything. It puts the photo in a pile you can look through, change your mind about, and only clear when you’re ready.

  1. Swipe down

    The photo goes into Siftory’s Trash. It’s still on your phone, untouched.

  2. Review

    See everything you’ve trashed in one place. Move anything back with a tap.

  3. Complete

    You confirm, then Android asks you to allow the change as well.

  4. Recycle bin, for 30 days

    Deleted items go to your phone’s Recycle bin, where Android normally keeps them for around 30 days, so you can still restore them.

Questions

Is anything deleted straight away?

No. Swiping down only puts an item in Siftory’s Trash. Nothing on your phone changes until you review your decisions and tap Complete, and Android asks you to allow the change too.

Where do deleted photos go, and can I get them back?

They go to your phone’s Recycle bin (on Android 11 and later), where Android normally keeps them for around 30 days. Restore them from Trash › Recently deleted in Siftory, or from the Recycle bin in your Gallery app.

What does Later do?

Later is for photos you’re not sure about yet. Nothing happens to them, and you can come back to them another time.

Are Collections real albums?

They can be. Link a Collection to a phone album and, when you complete a Sift, its photos are put into that album, so you’ll see it in your Gallery app. Collections you don’t link stay inside Siftory.

Are my photos uploaded? Do I need an account?

No and no. Everything happens on your phone, there’s no account to create, and Siftory has no servers that store your photos or videos. The privacy policy has the details.

What’s free, and what does Lifetime unlock?

The free version gives you 100 decisions to start, 30 more each month and up to 5 Collections. Siftory Lifetime is a one-off £4.99 payment for unlimited sifting and unlimited Collections. It will be available when Siftory launches on Google Play.

Will there be a subscription?

No. Lifetime is a single payment, not a subscription.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Siftory is for Android phones.
